A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How are ISAC.SI and ISAC.XP different? +

ISAC.SI is a structured learning platform for students and teams in MRDI programs. ISAC.XP helps people map and communicate their capability through an AI-guided profile. They focus on different parts of the learning journey.

Can I try ISAC.XP without joining an MRDI program? +

Yes. ISAC.XP is open to learners and professionals; start by building a free profile. ISAC.SI access is part of MRDI’s enrolled, structured programs.

What does the Learning Capability Index look at? +

The LCI organizes capability across six behavioral dimensions: Logic, Velocity, Stability, Consistency, Recall, and Confidence.

Can colleges or employers work with MRDI on ISAC? +

MRDI welcomes conversations with institutions and industry partners about structured learning and capability profiling. Get in touch to discuss your context.
