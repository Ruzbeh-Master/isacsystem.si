Make practical skills and experience visible.

ISAC.XP turns projects, workshop habits, self-learning, and real problem solving into a Learning Capability Index (LCI) profile—so learners can show what they can do beyond a marksheet.

Explore ISAC.XP →

Six dimensions of capability

Logic

How you reason through a challenge.

Velocity

How you move from understanding to action.

Stability

How you respond when work becomes uncertain.

Consistency

How reliably you repeat a sound process.

Recall

How you apply what you learned to real tasks.

Confidence

How you express and apply what you know.

For learners and recruiters

Learners can reflect on projects, practice, and self-learning. Recruiters can use the profile as a prompt to discuss a candidate’s examples and evidence.
