01 / THE LEARNING CAPABILITY INDEX

Capability is a pattern.
Not one test score.

LCI organizes practical signals across six dimensions to help learners reflect on their strengths and where focused practice can help.

01 / REASONLogic

How clearly you reason through a problem and choose your next step.

02 / MOVEVelocity

How quickly you move from understanding to useful action.

03 / ADAPTStability

How you respond when a task becomes uncertain or pressured.

04 / REPEATConsistency

How reliably you repeat a good process across attempts.

05 / APPLYRecall

How previous learning comes back when a real task demands it.

06 / EXPRESSConfidence

How accurately you express, support, and apply what you know.

05 / START YOUR CAPABILITY PROFILE

Bring your experience
into focus.

Prepare a clear story about what you have built, practiced, and learned. These steps explain what to gather before creating or discussing an ISAC.XP profile.

STEP 01 / COLLECTChoose real work.

Note projects, workshop tasks, repairs, prototypes, and things you learned independently.

STEP 02 / REFLECTDescribe your process.

Think about how you reason, respond to setbacks, keep a routine, and apply what you learned.

STEP 03 / SHOWKeep evidence nearby.

Gather project notes, photos, demos, and examples that help explain your contribution.
