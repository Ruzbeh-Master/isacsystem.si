02 / EMPLOYER PERSPECTIVES · 3 FORMS

What hiring teams shared

Plain-language summaries of the recruiter and industry feedback forms.

GREPL · RECRUITER FEEDBACK

Strong support for screening and bulk review

The recruiter rated score interpretation, candidate access, result summaries, and question relevance positively. Candidate screening, score generation, and bulk processing were highlighted as useful. The form reported no major technical issues and high overall satisfaction.

Requested next: integrations with hiring systems, bulk candidate import, API access, role-specific questions, custom score thresholds, and scheduling options. The respondent also asked the team to keep improving the product.

NANAVATI TOYOTA · INDUSTRY FEEDBACK

Useful AI evaluation, with clearer reports needed

The respondent valued AI-assisted candidate evaluation and shortlisting. Core areas were rated from average to good, and the form indicated that the system was understandable. Overall satisfaction was more measured than in the other employer forms.

Requested next: concise, point-by-point screening summaries and stronger support for bulk screening and selection workflows.

SAMRAT · RECRUITER FEEDBACK

A reliable fit for current hiring needs

The recruiter rated score generation, candidate access, result summaries, question quality, and bulk processing very highly. AI-powered candidate evaluation and shortlisting were called out as valuable, and the form reported no confusing areas or recurring technical issues.

Looking ahead: continue adding advanced AI features while maintaining the platform’s reliable, efficient recruitment experience.

Employer responses are summarized from the submitted forms. Personal names, email addresses, signatures, and other contact fields are omitted.
